Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-03

This Privacy Policy describes how Walking Cooler (the “Application”) collects, uses, and protects information when used by employees of Acuity Analytics (the “Service Provider”).

The Application is intended solely for internal company use and is provided “AS IS”.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you use the Application, we collect the following information:

  • Work email address
  • Name

This information is used to identify you within the Application and associate activity data with the correct user account. Personal email addresses are not collected or supported.

Fitness and Activity Data

The Application collects step count data generated through Google Fit or Apple Health.

This data is used to:

  • Display your activity within the Application
  • Support internal challenges

We do not collect medical data, heart rate data, or precise health diagnostics.

Automatically Collected Information

The Application may collect limited technical information automatically, such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage data (e.g., feature interaction, crash logs)

This information is used strictly for maintenance, debugging, and performance improvements.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No precise GPS or real-time location data
  • No personal contacts
  • No photos, media, or microphone data
  • No data for advertising or marketing purposes

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Operate and maintain the Application
  • Display user-specific activity and progress
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Support internal company wellness initiatives

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared for commercial purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal or fitness data with third parties, except:

  • When required by law
  • With trusted service providers who help operate the Application and are bound by confidentiality obligations

All shared data, if any, is limited to what is strictly necessary.

Data Retention

  • User account information (name and work email) is retained while you are an active user of the Application.
  • Fitness and activity data is retained only as long as necessary for Application functionality and internal reporting.

If you leave the company or request deletion, your data will be removed within a reasonable timeframe unless retention is required for legal or operational reasons.

Your Rights and Choices

You may request:

  • Access to your data
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of your data, where applicable

Requests can be made by contacting us at walkingcooler@ascent.io.

You may also stop all data collection by uninstalling the Application.

Children’s Privacy

The Application is intended for adult employees only and is not designed for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information. Access is limited to authorized personnel only.

While we take data protection seriously, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the Application or made available to users. Continued use of the Application indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
