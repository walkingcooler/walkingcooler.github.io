Relevant Legal Bases

Relevant legal bases under the GDPR: Below you will find an overview of the legal bases of the GDPR on which we process personal data. Please note that, in addition to the GDPR, national data protection regulations may apply.

  • Performance of a contract and pre-contractual inquiries (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) – Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract or participation in the internal application.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) – Processing is necessary to operate, secure, and improve the internal application, provided that the interests or fundamental rights of the data subjects do not override these interests.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) – In particular for connecting Google Fit or Apple Health to retrieve step data.

General Information on Data Storage and Deletion

We delete personal data in accordance with legal requirements as soon as the underlying consent is revoked or there are no other legal bases for processing. This applies in particular when the purpose of processing no longer applies or the data is no longer required.

Exceptions apply if statutory retention obligations or legitimate interests require longer storage.

  • 10 years – Commercial and tax law documents
  • 8 years – Accounting records
  • 6 years – Other business documents
  • 3 years – Data relevant for potential legal claims (statutory limitation periods)

Provision of the Online Offer and Hosting

We process users’ data in order to provide our online services. For this purpose, we process the user’s IP address, which is necessary to transmit the content and functions of our online services to the user’s browser or device.

Access to our application is logged in so-called server log files. These may include IP addresses, date and time of access, pages accessed, and technical information about the device and browser.

Log data is stored for a maximum of 30 days and then deleted or anonymized, unless further retention is required for security or legal reasons.

Registration, Login, and User Account

Users can create a user account. During registration, the required mandatory information is communicated and processed for the purpose of providing the user account. This includes in particular:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Login credentials

In addition, we process step data (step counts) retrieved via Google Fit or Apple Health, provided that the user has connected these services and given consent.

Step data is used exclusively to:

  • Display activity within the application
  • Calculate and display rankings and challenges
  • Enable participation in internal step challenges

The application is intended solely for internal use by employees. Data is not used for advertising purposes and is not sold.
