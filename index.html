Walking Cooler

Turn your daily steps into a team victory

Walking Cooler is the official fitness and community app designed exclusively for the employees of Acuity Analytics. We're extending the "Water Cooler" outside, helping you stay active, connect with colleagues, and engage in some healthy competition. Whether you're going for a walk during lunch break or running a half marathon, every step counts towards your team's success.

Why Walking Cooler?

✨ Step Into Wellbeing

Enhance your workday with effortless step tracking designed to prioritize your health. Every step matters, and we make it easy to track your progress.

👥 Team Up for Success

You'll never walk alone! Form teams with your best buddies at work to tackle challenges together and motivate each other to reach new heights.

🏆 The Ultimate Leaderboard

Track your progress in real-time. See which teams and people are leading and who you have to pass to earn that #1 spot in the company rankings.

Features Built for You

🔐 Seamless Work Login

Sign in quickly and securely using your official work email. Get started in seconds and access all features instantly.

🎯 Live Challenges

Participate in seasonal company-wide walking events and challenges. Stay engaged with fresh, exciting competitions throughout the year.

📊 Everything Counts

By retrieving step data from Google Fit, your steps always get updated – no matter which device you use (Smartphone, Smartwatch, or similar).
